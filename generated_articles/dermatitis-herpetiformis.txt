        "DH is the specific cutaneous manifestation of celiac disease/gluten-sensitive enteropathy. Most, but not all, affected patients have circulating anti-tTG antibodies and duodenal villous atrophy; approximately 70% have villous atrophy, while others have celiac-like inflammatory intestinal changes. Therefore, absence of diarrhea, weight loss, or malabsorption symptoms should not alter the need for a lifelong gluten-free diet after diagnostic confirmation. [1][17][22]",
